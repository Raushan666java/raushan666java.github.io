    🗺️ 3 Month Roadmap¶

    Start: June 8, 2026 | End: September 7, 2026 Daily: 5 hours | Weekend: Full day

    Ground Rules¶

    Follow These — No Exceptions

    1. Padhte padhte banao — har concept ke saath code
    2. Har week 1 working thing GitHub pe push hona chahiye
    3. Stuck? 30 min try → phir Google → phir poochho
    4. No tutorial hell — ek resource, usse complete karo
    5. Consistency > Intensity — 5 hrs daily > 10 hrs weekend only
